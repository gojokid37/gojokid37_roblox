⚔️ Combat Fundamentals

Understanding the flow of a fight is more important than simply acting constantly.

⚔️ Attack Awareness

Don't act automatically. Pay attention to spacing, openings and what your opponent is doing.

🛡️ Defensive Awareness

Defensive decisions are about staying calm, recognizing pressure and choosing the right response.

⏱️ Timing

Good timing can completely change an interaction. Avoid rushing decisions just because the fight is moving quickly.

🏃 Movement & Positioning

Movement changes spacing, positioning and how predictable your decisions become.

🏃 Movement

Purposeful movement helps control spacing and makes your decisions less predictable.

📐 Spacing

Understanding distance helps you make better decisions instead of constantly moving without a purpose.

🌀 Mix It Up

Repeating exactly the same movement pattern makes you easier to anticipate.

🧠 Things That Can Change a Fight

The small decisions are often what separate random play from deliberate play.

🛡️ Parry Timing

Don't panic. Watch the opponent's action and make your defensive response deliberately.

🎯 Prediction

If an opponent repeatedly makes the same choice, you can start recognizing the pattern.

⏳ Patience

Sometimes waiting gives you more information than immediately forcing an interaction.

🔄 Adaptation

If your current approach isn't working, change it. Repeating the same mistake teaches your opponent your pattern.

⚡ Reactions

Fast reactions help, but understanding what you're reacting to is just as important.

🦘 Jumping

Jumping changes your position and can make your movement less predictable.

🏆 Ranked Mindset

Ranked play is about consistency, learning and adapting.

📈 Consistency

Focus on making repeatable decisions instead of trying to win every situation with a risky move.

🧠 Learn From Losses

After a match, identify one decision you could have handled differently. One useful lesson is better than simply blaming the opponent.

🎯 Stay Adaptable

Every opponent can create a different problem. Don't force one strategy into every situation.

❌ Beginner Mistakes

Avoiding these habits can make improvement much easier.

❌ Panic Attacking

Constant action can make your decisions predictable. Take moments to observe.

❌ Ignoring Movement

Positioning matters. Standing in the same place can make spacing easier for the opponent to control.

❌ No Adaptation

If something isn't working, change your approach instead of repeating it.

❌ Rushing

A rushed decision can turn a manageable situation into a difficult one.
