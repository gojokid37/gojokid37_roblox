🔥 Advanced Knowledge

DON'T JUST FIGHT — READ YOUR OPPONENT.

🧠 READ THE PATTERN

If someone repeatedly attacks after approaching, remember that pattern. If someone constantly retreats after an exchange, expect the retreat. If someone repeatedly chooses the same movement direction, adapt to it.

1️⃣ Observe

Watch what your opponent repeatedly does before deciding how to respond.

2️⃣ Test

Try a different approach and see how they react.

3️⃣ Adapt

Once you identify a pattern, change your decisions around it.

4️⃣ Stay Unpredictable

Remember that your opponent is watching you too. Don't fall into your own repeated patterns.

🎮 Beginner Mistakes

❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do I get better at Duelling Grounds?

Practice movement, timing, observation and adaptation. Focus on learning from each encounter.

Is speed everything?

No. Positioning, timing, prediction and decision-making are also important.

Why do I keep losing the same way?

You may be repeating the same pattern. Look at what happened immediately before the mistake and try changing it.

Should I always attack first?

Not necessarily. Sometimes waiting allows you to learn what your opponent wants to do.

How can I become less predictable?

Avoid repeating the exact same movement and decision sequence every time.
