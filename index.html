🧠 Things You Might Not Know

Small details can change how you approach a fight.

TIP 01

🛡️ Parry Timing

Good timing matters more than panic-clicking. Watch the opponent and respond when an attack is actually coming.

TIP 02

🦘 Jumping

Movement options can make your actions harder to predict. Use movement deliberately instead of randomly.

TIP 03

⚡ Powerful Attacks

Don't automatically panic when an opponent uses a powerful move. Stay aware and look for an appropriate response.

TIP 04

🎯 Prediction

Experienced players don't only react. They observe patterns and anticipate what may happen next.

TIP 05

⏱️ Patience

Sometimes waiting is better than forcing an action. Let your opponent create an opening.

TIP 06

🧠 Adaptation

If your approach isn't working, change it. Repeating the same idea gives your opponent information.

🔥 Advanced Knowledge

Don't just react. Learn to recognize patterns.

DON'T JUST FIGHT — READ YOUR OPPONENT.

If someone repeatedly chooses the same response, remember the pattern.

If someone always moves in the same direction, expect that possibility and adjust your decision.

If an opponent waits for you to attack, don't give them the exact situation they are expecting.

If your opponent suddenly changes their approach, don't keep using the same response automatically.

Watch habits. Good players can become predictable when they repeat the same pattern.

The strongest advantage isn't always speed. Sometimes it is understanding what the opponent is likely to do next.

🎮 Beginner Mistakes

Avoid these habits and you'll already be thinking differently from a beginner.

MISTAKE 01

❌ Panic Attacking

Constantly attacking can make your next move predictable.

MISTAKE 02

❌ Ignoring Movement

Movement can create opportunities while helping you avoid pressure.

MISTAKE 03

❌ No Adaptation

If something isn't working, change your approach instead of repeating it.

MISTAKE 04

❌ Rushing

A rushed decision can turn a good situation into a bad one.
