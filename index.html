🔥 Combat Fundamentals

Winning isn't simply about pressing buttons faster. Good combat comes from understanding when to attack, defend, move and wait.

⚔️ Read the Fight

Pay attention to what your opponent does before committing to your next move. Repeated habits can become predictable.

A strong player isn't necessarily the player attacking constantly. Good decision-making can create openings without forcing them.

🛡️ Defense & Timing

Defensive mechanics are just as important as offense. Learning when to defend can stop an opponent's pressure and give you an opportunity to respond.

Don't panic when an opponent attacks. Watch the timing and make your decision deliberately.

🏃 Movement

Movement changes the spacing between you and your opponent. Avoid becoming completely predictable.

Mix your positioning with your attacks and defensive options instead of following one repeated pattern.

🧠 Mechanics You Should Understand

Small improvements in your understanding can make fights feel completely different.

🛡️ Parry Timing

Don't panic when an attack comes toward you. Learn the timing and react deliberately instead of repeatedly pressing the defensive button.

🏃 Movement

Movement affects spacing, positioning and how easy you are to predict.

🎯 Prediction

Watch for repeated habits. If an opponent keeps making the same choice, you can prepare for it.

⏱️ Patience

Sometimes waiting for an opponent to commit creates a better opportunity than attacking immediately.

🧠 Adaptation

If your current approach isn't working, change it. Repeating the same mistake gives your opponent information.

⚡ Reactions

Fast reactions help, but understanding what you are reacting to is just as important.

🔥 Advanced Tips

The difference between randomly fighting and actually learning from fights is awareness.

01 — Watch Patterns

If your opponent repeatedly attacks after closing distance, start expecting that pattern.

02 — Don't Become Predictable

If you always react the same way, your opponent can begin anticipating you.

03 — Control Your Pace

You don't have to attack every second. Sometimes changing the pace of the fight gives you more information.

04 — Learn From Every Loss

Instead of only thinking about the result, identify the moment where the fight started going wrong.

05 — Don't Panic

A bad situation can become worse when you make a rushed decision. Stay focused and look for the next opportunity.

🎮 Beginner Mistakes

Avoiding these habits can help you improve faster.

❌ Panic Attacking

Constantly attacking without thinking can make your actions easy to predict.

❌ Ignoring Movement

Standing in one predictable position can make it easier for an opponent to control the fight.

❌ Never Adapting

If something isn't working, change your approach instead of repeating it.

❌ Rushing

A rushed decision can turn a manageable situation into a bad one.

❌ Ignoring Your Opponent

Don't focus only on your own attacks. Watch what the other player is preparing to do.

❌ Getting Frustrated

A frustrating match can become a learning opportunity if you look at what actually happened.
