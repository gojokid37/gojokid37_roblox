GAMEPLAY

KNOW THE EXPERIENCE

Understand the environment before worrying about advanced play.

🎮 Enter The Arena

Take time to understand the arena layout, movement space and how players interact with the environment.

🕹️ Learn The Controls

Knowing where your controls are makes the experience smoother and lets you focus more on what is happening around you.

🧠 Build Awareness

Pay attention to your surroundings instead of focusing on only one thing happening directly in front of you.

MOVEMENT

MOVE WITH PURPOSE

Movement is one of the easiest areas to improve through practice.

🏃 Stay Mobile

Avoid becoming completely predictable. Learn how your character moves and become comfortable changing direction.

🎯 Control Your Position

Know where you are in the arena. Positioning can affect how many options you have when the situation changes.

🔄 Change Direction

Practice moving in different directions instead of following the exact same movement pattern every time.

⚡ Stay Comfortable

Good movement should eventually feel natural instead of requiring you to think about every individual input.

MENTAL GAME

ARENA AWARENESS

The strongest improvement isn't always faster reactions. Sometimes it is simply noticing more.

👀

Watch Patterns

Notice repeated movement and habits. Players often develop patterns without realizing it.

🧠

Stay Patient

You don't have to react instantly to everything. Take a moment to understand what is happening.

🔄

Adapt

If something isn't working, don't repeat it endlessly. Change your approach and learn from the result.

📍

Know Your Position

Always know where you are in relation to the center, edges and available space.

⏱️

Improve Timing

Practice recognizing the right moment to move, defend, reset or reposition.

🔥

Stay Calm

A calm player can usually notice more information than someone who is rushing every decision.

PROGRESSION

FROM BEGINNER TO EXPERIENCED

A simple progression system for improving your overall gameplay.

1

Learn The Basics

Understand the controls, camera, movement and basic game flow before worrying about advanced techniques.

2

Get Comfortable

Play enough matches that moving around the arena and using your controls starts becoming natural.

3

Study Your Mistakes

Instead of only thinking about whether you won or lost, think about what caused important moments in the match.

4

Recognize Patterns

Start noticing repeated behaviors, movement habits and situations that happen frequently.

5

Adapt

Once you recognize a pattern, experiment with a different response and see what happens.

6

Build Your Own Style

Eventually your movement, awareness and decision-making become your own personal playstyle.
