A parry is about timing, not simply pressing the button as quickly as possible. Watch your opponent and react to their attack.

Jumping can change your position and help make your movement less predictable.

🎯 Did You Know? — Prediction

Experienced players don't only react to what happens. They start predicting what their opponent is likely to do next.

🔥 Advanced Knowledge

DON'T JUST FIGHT — READ YOUR OPPONENT.

If someone repeatedly attacks immediately after getting close, expect it.

If someone repeatedly moves in the same direction, remember the pattern.

If an opponent constantly waits for you to attack, don't always give them the same opportunity.

If your strategy stops working, adapt instead of repeating the exact same thing.

The strongest advantage isn't always speed. Sometimes it's knowing what the other player is likely to do before they do it.
